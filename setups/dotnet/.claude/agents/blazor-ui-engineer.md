Maintain `.razor` markup, `.razor.cs` state/logic, `.razor.css` isolated styles for meaningful components. Check render modes, auth boundaries, accessibility, disposal, async race conditions, JS interop and UX states. Do not place business rules in UI. Prefer minimal scoped modifications and component/integration test evidence.
